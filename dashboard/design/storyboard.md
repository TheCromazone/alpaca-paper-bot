# Storyboard — Overview page ("the machine's heartbeat")

## Site-wide cinematic grammar
- Page shell: full-bleed atmosphere (aurora + grid + grain + vignette) with a 1440px stage.
- Navigation posture: quiet mono pills under the masthead (unchanged position, refined).
- Framing: the fold belongs to ONE shot — the equity curve. Everything else is telemetry.
- Density cadence: monumental → dense corridor → ledger → wire (spectacle / dense / breathing / dense).
- Recurring atmosphere: film grain (≤2.5% opacity), vignette, existing aurora + scanline.

## Visual thesis (one line)
A single luminous heartbeat — the equity curve — commands the fold; the rest of the page
is the machine's telemetry, staged as numbered scenes.

## Hero dominance statement
The stage is expensive because the chart bleeds edge-to-edge inside one deep panel with the
ops HUD fused to its top edge and the scorecard fused to its bottom edge — chart, status and
verdict become one instrument instead of three stacked widgets. No gradient-hero clichés:
the depth comes from layered atmosphere, count-up numerals, and the draw-in curve.

## Acts (scene theses)
- **ACT 01 — THE STAGE** (signature composition, irreplaceable): full-bleed panel;
  ops HUD strip (bot heartbeat · regime · next routine · burn) fused on top; 560px equity
  curve center; performance verdict rail (alpha vs S&P) fused underneath. One instrument.
- **ACT 02 — THE MACHINE**: the Routine Reel — LLM runs as film frames on a horizontal
  strip (sprocket-hole edges, status glow, cost + tool count per frame). New component.
- **ACT 03 — THE PLAYBOOK**: today's thesis (research log) + manual override + earnings
  watch, asymmetric 7/5 split.
- **ACT 04 — THE BOOK**: sector exposure + top of the book / under the stop ledgers.
- **ACT 05 — THE WIRE**: orders + headlines + insider flow (6/4/2 asymmetry, kept).

## Entrance map (≥4 distinct types; fadeUp budget ≤2/page)
1. ACT 01: stage clip-reveal upward + chart draw-in + numeral count-up (composite entrance).
2. Ops HUD cards: stagger rise (this is fadeUp use #1 of 2).
3. ACT 02: reel frames slide in from the right, staggered — film advancing.
4. Scene heads: text wipe (clip-path inset reveal left→right).
5. ACT 04: ledgers slide-in-left, staggered.
6. ACT 05: blur-in.
All gated behind IntersectionObserver (Reveal component); disabled under
prefers-reduced-motion (also kills aurora/scanline/marquee/grain drift).
